Privacy policy

How Upchar SoftDesk handles the information on this website and in the software we provide to hospitals and clinics. Last updated 25 November 2026.

This is a draft, written to describe what our website and software actually do. It will be reviewed by our advisers and updated once our company registration is complete.

Who we are

Upchar SoftDesk (Medisoft Manager) is a Lucknow-based team that develops and supports software for clinics, dental clinics, nursing homes, hospitals and diagnostic centres. Our company registration is in progress; the registered name and address will be added here when it is complete.

This website

  • We do not use cookies, tracking or advertising tools, and this website does not ask for an account.
  • Forms are not stored by us. The call-back, feedback and "get the demo link" forms only prepare a WhatsApp message on your own device. Nothing is sent until you press send in WhatsApp, and then it reaches us as a normal WhatsApp message.
  • Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, and the website is hosted by Render. Like any website, these services receive your device's internet address when the page loads.
  • The live demos contain only made-up patients. Please do not enter real patient details in a demo. Everything entered there is deleted automatically, at the latest every night.

Patient information in the software we provide

  • The hospital or clinic decides. Each hospital or clinic decides what patient information it records and why. Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, it is responsible for that information, and we handle it only on its behalf and on its instructions.
  • Consent is recorded. The hospital software records the patient's consent to digital records, with who took it and when.
  • Separate database for each customer. Every hospital or clinic has its own separate database. One customer's patients are never visible to another.
  • Where it is kept. Databases are hosted with Neon and the software runs on Render, both in their Singapore region. Encrypted nightly backups are kept on GitHub for 30 days.
  • Protection. All connections use HTTPS. Staff and owners use separate logins, wrong passwords lock an account for a while, and backups are encrypted with a passphrase held by us.
  • Who can see it. Only the hospital's own logins, and our team when the hospital asks us for support or when needed to keep the system running and backed up. We do not sell or share patient information, and we do not use it for advertising.
  • Your copy. A hospital or clinic can download a full copy of its data at any time. If it stops using our service, we hand over a complete copy and then delete its database and backups.

Your rights

Patients who want to see, correct or remove their information should contact the hospital or clinic that recorded it; we will help that hospital or clinic act on the request. For anything about this website or our service, contact us using the details below.

Questions doctors ask us

Do we need to install anything or buy a server?

No. It opens in the browser on any computer, tablet or phone. We run the servers, backups and updates.

Who can see our patients' data?

Only the logins you create. Each hospital has its own separate database, and staff logins cannot see revenue or the owner's panel.

What happens if the internet goes down?

The system needs internet, but very little: a mobile hotspot is enough. Nothing is lost, because every saved entry is already on the server.

Can we change our prices and beds ourselves?

Yes. The owner can edit the whole price list and the beds at any time, confirming each change with their password.

Is it ready for ABDM and ABHA?

It records patients' ABHA numbers and consent, and prints your HFR facility ID and each doctor's registration and HPR ID on slips and bills.

What if we want to stop using it?

You pay monthly with no lock-in. We give you a complete copy of all your data.
